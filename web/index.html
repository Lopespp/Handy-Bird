How to play

Open hand, free flight

Hand up, bird up; hand down, bird down. No gravity and no jumping: Handy follows your palm.

Make a fist

Fires a laser at the bird's height. It breaks the blockers that close some pipe gaps and takes down the boss's minions that cross the screen.

Lives and power-ups

You have 3 lives. Power-ups show up in random spots, sometimes inside a pipe gap: a 10-second shield, slow motion and a triple laser.

Three bosses

Each map ends with its boss: Valley Warden, Sharking and Ironbeak. Dodge the attacks and hit the boss with your laser until its health runs out. Beat all three to win.

  • ✌️Pause with your hand: raise your index and middle fingers and fold the other three. Make a fist to keep going.
  • Good light in front of you and your whole palm in view.
  • Use Calibrate height to match how far your hand can reach.
  • Your video never leaves your computer: the hand is recognized right in the browser.
